FAQ

支持哪些水印？Which watermarks are supported?

白色/浅色文字水印（含各类 AI 工具水印）全自动检测；深色、彩色、logo 等其它样式用"框选水印"手动框住即可擦除。

White/light text watermarks are auto-detected; dark, colored or logo watermarks can be removed by manually boxing them.

采用 LaMa 修复模型按背景纹理智能补全，只处理水印区域。处理前有预览、处理后有对比，不满意可取消或重试。

LaMa reconstructs only the watermark area. Preview before and compare after; cancel anytime.

Local inference is what keeps your images private. One-time download only.
